### listened - [202605 - listening](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/1aXLTvo8gzbMGNYRTPcAau?si=ddf3414980c74663)

- [saxophone collosus](https://sonny-rollinsvw.bandcamp.com/album/saxophone-colossus) by sonny rollins
    - trying to fill an embarassing blind spot spurred by this loss. it's as good as anyone would tell you; potentially better.
- [middle of nowhere](https://kaceymusgraves.com/collections/vinyl/products/middle-of-nowhere-sand-colored-vinyl) by kacey musgraves
    - musgraves holds a particular place in my heart for clever, interesting, and honest (though tongue-and-cheeck) song writing. the album is pretty much all killer, and i'd highly recommend minimally checking out 'dry spell' and 'mexico honey'.
- [american football (lp 4)](https://americanfootball.bandcamp.com/album/american-football-lp4) by american football
    - second 'best' american football record to date, including some absolutely incredible drumming from lamos and gorgeous atmospheric synths from (i assume) nate kinsella. the song writing is strong, i think, though i'm starting to suspect this is more of a lies/owen album than a 'proper' american football album (complimentary). bummed to see that they're not playing 'no soul to save' in the current setlist, but these tracks surely augment an already incredible catalog and live show.
- [that's how we choose to remember it](https://rilokiley.bandcamp.com/album/thats-how-we-choose-to-remember-it) by rilo kiley
    - had the absolute privilege of seeing rilo kiley at the chicago theatre and revisted the discography in preparation. i came to the conclusion that this band was wholly under-appreciated at its supposed height, and i'm so glad they're getting their flowers now. live, the band is tight; lewis has bonkers good stage presence as she switches between bass, guitar, and keys; they sound like they never stopped. justice for 'monkeymaker' and 'fold[ing] in half so easily'
