    - long on the list of things to read, the first 50 pages reminded me what a certain kind of nonfiction is capable of achieving. the problem i ended up having about 2/3rds in was seeing the forest rather than the trees, and i think abdurraqib operates much more successfully at tree level. the arc of the narrative basically didn't work for me even if i liked being lead along the path. that said, we happy to spend more and different time with a writer i really admire and appreciate
